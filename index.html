    Step 4
    
Where it is weak

    
Most trainers give you one number and let you believe it. These views exist to contradict that
      number whenever it deserves contradicting.

  

  






  

  

    Four ways to break it on purpose — each gives a model that scores beautifully and is worthless
    

    

    

      
      The background did it
        Record one class on the left of the room and the other on the right. The score
          will look superb. Then open what it keyed on, and watch the weight settle on the wall behind
          you.
    

    

      
      Five examples is not enough
        Record five per class. Training accuracy will read near perfect and the
          held-back score will not. The distance between those two numbers is the size of the lie.
    

    

      
      One class shouting over another
        Give one class sixty examples and another eight. Accuracy stays high because it
          learned to guess the common one, and the confusion matrix shows the rare class collapsing into
          it.
    

    

      
      It has never heard of nothing
        Train two poses, then strike a third it has never seen. It will answer
          confidently anyway. A classifier cannot say it does not know unless you give it a class for
          that.
